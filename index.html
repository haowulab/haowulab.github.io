Computational Biology & Bioinformatics

Wu Laboratory 吴浩实验室

"Developing statistical methods for the era of biomedical big data"

We develop innovative computational tools to decode complex biomedical big data. Our core research integrates multi-omics (bulk, single-cell, and spatial) and epigenomics to address challenges in oncology and neurology. Beyond omics, we leverage EHR and wearable sensor data to build predictive models for precision health and personalized treatment.
